# Water Change — "Dump"

Reference for completing a water change on a hot tub.

Typical reason for dumping a pool:
- **High TDS:** dirty water from high usage
- **High Chlorine:** chlorine level in pool is abnormally high and/or Combined Chlorine is over 1.0 and a backwash has been preformed and was ineffective.
- **Routine Maintenance:** any work which requires the pool to be dry

## Before You Dump

**Turn OFF chemicals and controller:** dumping a pool with the controller on and the chemicals flowing is a waste. Also, in the case of the Outdoor Large, turning OFF and ON the pump without closing the Chlorine supply can cause the puck tube to vacuum seal.

## Dump Procedure

1. Open the WASTE line and close the SKIMMER line
2. Turn OFF the pump
3. Turn the 6-Way valve **clockwise** to WASTE
4. Turn ON the pump
5. Set a timer. All hot tubs drain at different rates. Consult the chart for approximate timings.
6. Close the hot tub by putting up a sign

## Fill Procedure

Once the water level is just above the drain, it is time to fill the hot tub.

1. Turn OFF the pump
2. Turn the 6-Way valve **clockwise** to FILTER
3. Turn ON the pump
4. Turn ON the fill valve(s). All hot tubs have a water fill valve located in the pool mechanical room. The indoor hot tub and outdoor small hot tub also have a fill valve on the pool deck.
5. Set a timer. All hot tubs fill at different rates. Consult the chart for approximate timings.
6. Fill the hot tub to halfway on the skimmer inlet
7. Turn OFF the fill valve(s)

## Before You Re-Open

Pool chemistry must be balanced before the chemicals and pool controller are turned back on and the pool re-opened.

1. Test Alkalinity and adjust
2. Test Chlorine and adjust
3. Turn ON the controller but leave **all** chemicals OFF
3. Wait for hot tub to come up to 35 degrees
4. Test Chlorine and pH
5. Calibrate controller
6. Turn ON chemicals
7. Remove sign and open the pool
