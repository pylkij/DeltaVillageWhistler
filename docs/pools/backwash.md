# Backwash — Sand Filter Maintenance

Reference for the maintenance procedure of backwashing a sand filter for a hot tub and the pool.

Reasons for a backwash:
- **Weekly:** all filters must be backwashed at least once a week
- **Low Flow:** if the flow readings are low
- **High Pressure:** if the filter pressure values are high
- **High TDS:** if Total Dissolved Solids are above 1500 PPM. Do this before a pool dump
- **High Combined Chlorine:** value higher than 1.0 PPM. Do this before a pool dump

## Before You Dump

**Turn OFF chemicals and controller:** backwashing a sand filter with the controller on and the chemicals flowing is a waste. Also, in the case of the Outdoor Large, turning OFF and ON the pump without closing the Chlorine supply can cause the puck tube to vacuum seal.

## Backwash Procedure

1. Open the WASTE line and close the SKIMMER line
2. Turn OFF the pump
3. Turn the 6-Way valve **clockwise** to BACKWASH
4. Turn ON the pump
5. Wait 2 minutes
6. Turn OFF the pump
7. Turn the 6-Way valve **clockwise** to RINSE
8. Turn ON the pump
9. Wait 30 seconds
10. Turn OFF the pump
11. Turn the 6-Way valve **clockwise** to FILTER
12. Turn ON the pump
14. Close the WASTE line
13. Fill water to halfway on the skimmer inlet
14. Open SKIMMER line and turn off water
15. Turn ON controller and chemicals

## Notes

- If the pump has lost prime, see (pump priming)[pump-priming.html]
- Only spin the 6-Way valve **clockwise**
- Ensure chemicals and controller are OFF before turning OFF or ON a pump
